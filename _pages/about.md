I'm a first-year **M.S. student in Computer Science at [DGIST](https://www.dgist.ac.kr/)**, currently a researcher in the **Real-Time Computing Lab (RTCL)**. My work sits at the intersection of **real-time systems** and **efficient deep learning** — specifically, how to offload DNN inference from edge devices to servers so that results arrive both accurately *and* on time.

Right now I'm focused on **image-adaptive JPEG compression**: choosing the quality factor and quantization table per image, so that offloaded inference holds up even when the available network bandwidth keeps shifting. More broadly, I'm interested in systems that must reason about the trade-off between data fidelity, transmission cost, and end-to-end deadlines.
