I am a first-year M.S. student in Computer Science at DGIST, where I work in the Real-Time Computing Lab. My research centers on real-time systems, with a focus on DNN inference offloading between edge devices and servers under latency constraints. In particular, I study image-adaptive JPEG compression — optimizing the quality factor and quantization table per image — so that offloaded inference stays accurate and timely even when the available network bandwidth fluctuates. I am broadly interested in systems that must reason about the trade-off between data fidelity, transmission cost, and end-to-end deadlines.
